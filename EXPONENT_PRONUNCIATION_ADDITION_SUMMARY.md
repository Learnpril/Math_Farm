## 🎓 Pronunciation Guidelines Provided

### **Standard Format**

- **"to the power of"**: 2³ = "2 to the power of 3"
- **"to the -th power"**: 5⁴ = "5 to the 4th power"

### **Common Examples**

- **2³**: "2 to the 3rd power" or "2 cubed"
- **5⁴**: "5 to the 4th power"
- **10²**: "10 to the 2nd power" or "10 squared"

### **Special Cases**

- **Squared**: x² can be read as "x squared" or "x to the 2nd power"
- **Cubed**: x³ can be read as "x cubed" or "x to the 3rd power"
