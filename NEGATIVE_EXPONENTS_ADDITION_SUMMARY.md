## ✅ What Was Added

### **1. Theory Section Enhancement**

Added detailed explanation of negative exponents to the main content:

**Key Concepts Added:**

- **Definition**: a^{-n} = 1 / a^n (reciprocal relationship)
- **Intuitive Understanding**: "how many times to divide into 1"
- **Example**: 2^{-3} = 1 / (2 × 2 × 2) = 1/8
- **Pattern Connection**: Shows how 2³ = 8 and 2^{-3} = 1/8 are related
- **Cross-references**: Links to future Fractions Basics section
- **Mathematical Consistency**: Connects to inverse operations like division undoing addition

### **2. Updated LaTeX Expression**

**Before:**

```latex
2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8 \text{ and } 10^2 = 10 \times 10 = 100
```

**After:**

```latex
2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8 \text{ and } 2^{-3} = \frac{1}{2^3} = \frac{1}{8}
```

### **3. New Worked Example**

Added a complete worked example demonstrating negative exponents:

**Problem**: Calculate 3^{-2}
**Solution**: 3^{-2} = 1 / 3² = 1 / 9

**Step-by-step Process:**

1. Recognize negative exponent notation
2. Apply the reciprocal rule: a^{-n} = 1 / a^n
3. Calculate the positive exponent: 3² = 9
4. Take the reciprocal: 1/9

**Common Errors Addressed:**

- Confusing negative exponent with negative result
- Forgetting to take the reciprocal
