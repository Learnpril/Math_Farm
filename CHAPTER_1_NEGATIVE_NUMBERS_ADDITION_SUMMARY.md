## ✅ What Was Added

### **1. New Theory Section: "Introduction to Negative Numbers"**

Added a complete conceptual introduction covering:

**Key Concepts:**

- **Definition**: Numbers less than zero, written with minus sign (-)
- **Real-world examples**: Temperature below zero, debt, depths below sea level
- **Number line visualization**: Negative numbers extend left from zero
- **Zero as divider**: Neither positive nor negative, the boundary point
- **Comparison rules**: Negative numbers are smaller than positive numbers
- **Future connections**: Preparation for subtraction and advanced math

**Mathematical Foundation:**

- Number line representation: ...−3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3...
- Directional understanding: Right = increasing, Left = decreasing
- Relative positioning: Farther left = smaller value

### **2. New Worked Example**

Added comprehensive comparison problem:

**Problem**: "Compare these numbers and arrange them from smallest to largest: 5, -2, 0, -7, 3"
**Solution**: "-7, -2, 0, 3, 5"

**Step-by-step Process:**

1. Understand negative vs positive relationship
2. Compare negative numbers (farther from zero = smaller)
3. Position zero as boundary
4. Compare positive numbers normally
5. Arrange in complete order

**Error Prevention:**

- Addresses common misconception that -7 > -2
- Clarifies zero's position between negative and positive
- Reinforces that negative < positive always
