## ✅ New Visual Component Created

### **NegativeNumbersIntro.tsx**

A fully interactive React component that provides hands-on exploration of negative numbers.

## 🎨 Visual Features

### **1. Interactive Number Line**

- **Range**: -5 to +5 with clear visual representation
- **Clickable Numbers**: Students can click any number to explore it
- **Color Coding**:
  - Red: Negative numbers
  - Blue: Zero (neutral)
  - Green: Positive numbers
- **Visual Line**: Horizontal line with arrow showing direction
- **Direction Labels**: "Smaller (More Negative)" ← → "Larger (More Positive)"

### **2. Selected Number Analysis**

When a student clicks a number, they see:

- **Number Properties**: "X is a positive/negative number"
- **Position Description**: "X is Y steps to the left/right of zero"
- **Comparison**: "X > 0" or "X < 0" or "X = 0"
- **Distance**: "Distance from zero: Y units"

### **3. Real-World Examples Toggle**

Interactive button reveals practical applications:

- **Temperature**: "5°C above freezing" vs "-3°C below freezing"
- **Money**: "$5 in your account" vs "$3 debt/owed"
- **Elevation**: "5 meters above sea level" vs "3 meters below sea level"
- **Color-coded**: Green for positive, red for negative, blue for zero

### **4. Key Concepts Summary**

Three colored cards explaining:

- **Negative Numbers**: Less than zero, written with minus sign
- **Zero**: Neither positive nor negative, the dividing point
- **Positive Numbers**: Greater than zero, with or without + sign

### **5. Ordering Challenge**

Yellow highlighted box with the key rule:
"Numbers get larger as you move right and smaller as you move left"
Example: -5 < -2 < 0 < 3 < 5
